Dojo BBQ, The Gili Islands, And A Turtle

February 15, 2016·Josh Gray-Emmer

The Gili Islands are a short boat ride from Bali with no motorized vehicles — just horses, bikes, and your feet. The water is crystal clear turquoise and the snorkeling is some of the best in the world.

The highlight was snorkeling with sea turtles. Not one turtle, but three. They were enormous and completely unbothered by us floating alongside them.

Back at the Dojo we hosted our first BBQ, which quickly turned into a 40-person party. Within two weeks I know more people in Bali than I knew in my first year in DTLA.
