The Ultimate Guide to Happy Hours in Canggu

June 4, 2016·Josh Gray-Emmer

Six months in Canggu means I've done the research. Here's your definitive guide to happy hours in one of Bali's best neighborhoods.

Dojo Bali — cheap drinks are just always on the menu. Best place to meet interesting people.

Old Man's is the legendary Canggu beach bar. $2 Bintangs all day.

Betelnut — hidden gem that makes the best cocktails in Canggu for about $4. Happy hour 5-7pm every day.

Batu Bolong Beach — Cold Bintang is $1.50, and the sunsets are outrageous. This is my go-to at least twice a week.
