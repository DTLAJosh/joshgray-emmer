If This Is So Easy, Why Isn't Everyone Doing It?

November 20, 2015·Josh Gray-Emmer

That's a really really good question. Everything I've read makes it seem relatively painless. What am I missing?

Maybe nothing. I don't have a relationship or desire to have children. That puts me in a very small group of people who can choose to pursue life on their own terms.

So why isn't everyone doing this? Because it doesn't fit with the life they live or want to live. It does fit well with mine, so I'm going for it!
