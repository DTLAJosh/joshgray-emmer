Making Friends in Bali is Stupid Easy

February 3, 2016·Josh Gray-Emmer

There's something about living in an island paradise that makes everyone really happy and super friendly. It's day three and I've already made about a half dozen new expat friends. Bali Dinner Club is in the works.

The best part is that I've found a home base about 5 minutes walk from my villa -- a co-working space called Dojo Bali. Crazy fast wifi, cafe, pool, and adorable cats and dogs to play with.

The local food is delicious and so so so cheap. I'm spending about $6 a day on food. Massages are $7 for an hour. Uber drivers cost about 1/10th the LA price. It's almost too good to be true, but I promise you it is.
