6 Simple Tips For Saving Thousands In Singapore

June 19, 2016·Josh Gray-Emmer

Every two months in Bali you need to do a "visa run" -- leave the country and come back to reset your visa. I chose Singapore.

1. Fly AirAsia. The Denpasar-Singapore route is almost always under $80 round trip if you book a few weeks ahead.

2. Stay in Little India. Guesthouses for $30-50/night that are clean, safe, and perfectly located.

3. The hawker centers are the move. World-class food for $3-6 a dish.

4. The MRT will take you anywhere. Singapore's subway is one of the best in the world and costs pennies.

5. Gardens by the Bay is free (mostly). The Supertrees and outdoor gardens and nightly light show -- all free.

6. Stock up on things you can't get in Bali. Decent cheese, good wine, Western medicines. Singapore has it all.
