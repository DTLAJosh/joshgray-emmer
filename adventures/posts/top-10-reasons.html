Top 10 Reasons to Move to Bali

July 14, 2016·Josh Gray-Emmer

Seven months in, here are my top 10 reasons you should consider making the move:

1. The cost of living is absurdly low. $1500/month buys a genuinely luxurious life.

2. The weather is perfect. Warm year round.

3. The food is incredible. And cheap.

4. The expat community is extraordinary. Bali attracts an unusually interesting, creative, entrepreneurial group of people.

5. The nature is stunning. Volcanoes, rice paddies, tropical forest, coral reefs, sea turtles.

6. The Balinese people are genuinely kind. Their culture values community, ceremony, and hospitality.

7. It's safe. Very safe.

8. The internet is good enough at co-working spaces.

9. It will change your perspective permanently on money, time, and what's necessary.

10. You can always go back. Your old life will still be there. This one won't wait for you.
