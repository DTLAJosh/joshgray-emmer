Bali Money

Learn How To Transfer Money To Bali Without Getting SCREWED!

May 12, 2016·Josh Gray-Emmer

Don't Use Your Regular Bank

Your US bank will murder you on exchange rates and fees. On a $1,000 transfer you could lose $50-80 without realizing it.

Use Transferwise (Now Wise)

By far the best option. They use the mid-market exchange rate and charge a small transparent fee. On a $1,000 transfer I saved about $40 compared to my bank.

The ATM Game in Bali

BCA ATMs have the best rates and lowest fees of the local banks. Avoid standalone ATMs near tourist areas. Always say NO to "conversion" and let your card do the conversion at home.

Keep a Buffer

ATMs sometimes run out of cash on holidays. Keep a week's worth of rupiah on hand.
