The DTLA Dinner Club
15 years. Still going.

Every week for 15 years, Josh has opened his rooftop to 30 strangers — free, multi-course, chef-driven, open to any DTLA resident. No ticket price. No agenda. Just the belief that a neighborhood is only as strong as the connections between its people. It's become one of Downtown LA's most beloved institutions, and a living demonstration of what community strategy actually looks like in practice.

Background

A career built on community

1989–2001

Film, Sundance & USC Cinema

Invited to Sundance by Robert Redford at age 13. Wrote and produced a film that premiered there at 18. Trained at the USC School of Cinema Television and worked in feature film development at Landmark Entertainment.

Entertainment
2001–2002

AirParty → AP Consulting

Founded AirParty — parties on planes for executives and celebrities. Post-9/11 pivot into AP Consulting, running large-scale experiential events for Comcast, E! Entertainment, Skyy Vodka, and Microsoft.

Entrepreneurship
2002–2012

DTLA Consulting & Urban Pioneer

Moved to Downtown LA and never looked back. Joined boards at the Central City Association and Historic Downtown BID. Branded, marketed, and helped lease dozens of adaptive reuse residential towers over a decade.

Community
2010–present

The DTLA Dinner Club — 15 years and still going

Launched a free weekly rooftop dinner for 30 residents. Multi-course, chef-driven, open to anyone in the neighborhood. Over 750 dinners hosted, welcoming an estimated 22,000+ neighbors to his table. Expanded the model internationally to Bali. Still running every week today.

Community
2013–2014

NationBuilder — Inside the Machine

Joined NationBuilder — first in-house role in over a decade. Became a deep platform expert and certified agency partner. Appointed DTLA Advisor to their board after departing.

Tech
2014–present

BridGE DTLA — Full Service Political & Community Agency

Founded BridGE as the culmination of every skill built before it. Clients include Uber, LA Chamber, DLA Piper, LA LGBT Center, Harridge Development, and Atlas Capital Group. Delivered 6× the opposition's speakers to City Hall on the Crossroads Hollywood project.
